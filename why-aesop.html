How it works

Unlike a typical AI tool that generates a freeform answer to your question, AESOP runs your question through a structured, multi-step analytical workflow — directed and validated at every stage by an experienced human analyst. The result is a properly-structured, fully-referenced report that you can act on, share, and rely on.

Every report we produce includes a front cover, a contents page with hyperlinks, an executive summary, the full analysis, and a sources/references section. It's designed to be read by people who need to extract value quickly — not wade through 100 pages to find the point.

The question matters

The quality of the report is directly related to the quality of the question. The most useful questions are specific and contextual — "what's happening in the real estate investment market and how should I think about this particular segment given our constraints?" is far more powerful than "what will the market do?" If you're unsure how to frame your question, our form walks you through it, and we'll come back to you if we need clarification before we start.

How quickly will I receive my report?

Most reports are delivered within 2 working days of receiving your confirmed submission — and any clarifying information we need. Tailored reports, which incorporate your own data and context, may take a little longer; we will confirm an expected delivery window when we confirm your order.

If you have a specific deadline, let us know in your request and we will tell you upfront whether we can meet it. We would rather decline a request than deliver a rushed report that falls below our standard.
