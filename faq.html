Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AESOP Solutions reports, pricing, and the process.

Pricing & access
Why is AESOP available here at a fraction of the licence cost?

Licences for the underlying AESOP technology sell for $1 million or more. That works for large government departments and the biggest businesses — but it shuts out small businesses, non-profits, and countless organisations that could benefit just as much. So instead of asking you to license the technology, we run the reports on our own AESOP infrastructure and deliver the results to you directly, at prices designed to make the power of the system available to all.

What are the pricing tiers?

There are three tiers, all priced in USD:

Public — $2,000 / €1,850: Your anonymised report is added to our public report library one week after delivery. Includes the full report, sources, and executive summary.

Private — $5,000 / €4,600: Your report stays entirely private — it is never published. Includes one round of clarification and rework.

Tailored — $10,000 / €9,200: Built incorporating your own data and context for the most specific result. Private. Turnaround confirmed at order.

If you've had a private report produced with us and want a further related report, we charge 50% of the standard tier price.

Do I pay before or after the report is produced?

Payment is taken upfront via Stripe when you submit your request. Once we've received your submission, we may send you some clarifying questions — only when we genuinely need them, not as a standard step. We then produce and deliver your report.

The guarantee
What if I'm not happy with my report?

Every report is covered by our no-quibble, money-back value guarantee. We know that even a few thousand dollars or euros is a significant sum for many organisations, so if the report doesn't deliver value for you, just tell us — we'll process your refund immediately, no questions asked.

Do I have to explain why I want a refund?

No. We'd genuinely welcome hearing where we fell short so we can improve, but you're under no obligation to tell us. We trust our outputs to deliver, and we trust you to be honest with us.

The reports
Will this really work for a smaller organisation like mine?

We've seen AESOP be transformational for our largest clients, and we built this service precisely so that organisations of every size can experience the same. Our hope is that it proves just as transformational for you.

What does a report actually look like?

Every report is a properly-structured document with a front cover, a contents page with hyperlinks through to each section, an executive summary designed to get you to the right sections quickly, the full analysis, and a sources and references section at the end. You can download example reports from our examples section to see the format and depth of output.

What kinds of questions work best?

Focused, contextual questions produce significantly more useful reports than broad ones. The best questions tell us what you're trying to decide, the sector or market you're operating in, and any specific constraints or concerns.

For example: "Should we enter the Southeast Asian logistics market in the next 18 months? We're a B2B SaaS business primarily serving mid-market financial services, and our main concern is whether local regulations and competitor positioning make entry viable within our investment window."

Compare that to: "Is Southeast Asia a good market?" — which would produce a much less actionable result.

Our request form has two fields — one for your central question, one for amplifying detail — to help you structure this. If your question needs more context, we'll follow up before we start.

How long does it take?

We aim to deliver most reports within 2 working days of receiving your submission and any clarifying information we need. Tailored reports, which incorporate your own data, may take slightly longer. We'll confirm an expected delivery window when we confirm your order.

Is this just AI generating an answer, or is a human involved?

This is not an AI generating a freeform response to your question. AESOP is a library of over 50 analytical workflows, each built from real analytical practice developed across four decades of defence intelligence, military planning, and commercial analysis. When you submit a question, AESOP selects the right workflow for it — then runs it.

A human analyst directs, reviews, and validates every stage of that process. Nothing ships without analyst sign-off. The result is a structured, fully-referenced report that carries the rigour of specialist analytical expertise — not a plausible-sounding answer generated on the fly. The difference in output quality is significant, and it is the reason AESOP exists.

Are the reports referenced?

Yes. Every report includes a sources and references section so you can trace conclusions back to their basis. This is a requirement, not an option — it's part of what makes the reports usable in a professional or organisational context.

Can I use the report however I like?

Your report is for your own internal use. You may share it within your organisation. You may not resell it, publish it as your own work, or use it to create competing services without our written agreement. See our Terms & Conditions for the full detail.

What format is the report delivered in?

Reports are delivered as a Microsoft Word document (.docx) by email, along with a PDF version. Every report includes a front cover, a hyperlinked table of contents, an executive summary, the full analysis in numbered sections, a glossary of abbreviations, and a sources and references section. If you need a different format, let us know when you submit your request and we will do our best to accommodate you.

Can I request revisions?

Private and Tailored tier reports include one round of clarification and rework as standard. This covers cases where the report has not fully addressed your question or where you need a specific section developed further. It does not cover requests to change the analytical conclusions, or requests that amount to a substantially different question. If you need more than one round of revision, we will discuss what is possible and whether it falls within the original scope or constitutes additional work.

Is there a confidentiality agreement or NDA available?

Our standard Terms and Conditions include confidentiality obligations on both sides: we do not share your question, context, or report with any third party, and your information is used solely for the purpose of producing your report. If you require a formal NDA before submitting, please contact us and we will arrange one before you proceed.

Can I cite or reference the report externally?

Yes, subject to the terms of your tier. You may cite the report and reference its findings in your own work, presentations, or publications, provided you attribute it to AESOP Solutions. You may not reproduce or republish the report in full, or use it as the basis for a competing analytical service. If you need broader usage rights — for example, to include the report in a client deliverable or published research — please contact us to discuss a suitable arrangement.

What topics or industries does AESOP cover?

AESOP Solutions can address analytical questions across virtually any topic or industry — from investment thesis validation and market entry analysis to regulatory exposure, competitive positioning, and policy research. The platform was originally developed for defence and national security intelligence, but its analytical methodology applies equally to commercial, financial, and policy questions. If you are unsure whether your question is a good fit, get in touch and we will tell you honestly whether we can add value.

What happens if I need to cancel after submitting but before delivery?

Because each report is produced specifically for your question and analytical work begins promptly after your submission is confirmed, we are not able to offer cancellation once production has started. However, our 7-day money-back guarantee means that if the completed report does not deliver value, you can request a full refund within 7 days of delivery — no questions asked. If you have a genuine reason to cancel before we have started work, please contact us as soon as possible and we will do what we reasonably can.

Data & privacy
Where is my data held?

Your data — including your question, any supporting context, and your completed report — is processed securely and used solely for the purpose of producing your report. We take data protection seriously and handle all personal data in accordance with UK GDPR. Payment data is processed by Stripe under its own terms.

See our Privacy Policy for full details of what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-DSS-compliant payment provider. We do not store your card details. Stripe processes payment data under its own terms as a data processor.
