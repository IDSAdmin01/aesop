What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

What cookies does this site use?

We use only strictly necessary cookies on this website. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or any other non-essential cookies.

Why no analytics cookies?

We have deliberately chosen not to use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) that require non-essential cookies. This means we do not need to ask for your consent before setting cookies, and your browsing of this site is not tracked for analytical purposes.

If this changes in future, we will update this policy and introduce a cookie consent mechanism before setting any non-essential cookies.

Strictly necessary cookies and consent

Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), strictly necessary cookies do not require your consent. However, we are required to disclose them — which this policy does.

Third-party cookies

When you proceed to payment, you are redirected to Stripe's hosted Checkout page. Stripe may set its own cookies as part of the payment process. These are governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy. We have no control over Stripe's cookies.

How to manage cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent the payment process from working correctly. For guidance on managing cookies in your browser, visit aboutcookies.org.

Changes to this policy

If we add new cookies or change our approach, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above.
